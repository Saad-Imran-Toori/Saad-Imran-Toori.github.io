About

I build full-stack applications with AI inside them, and I test them hardest where I most want them to be right.

I'm Saad. I'm in my final year of a Software Engineering degree at Capital University of Science & Technology in Islamabad, finishing in 2027. I started out building things for course projects — a desktop app in Java, a set of automated tests, a website for hosting games that ended up with real users — and I usually kept going after the assignment was done. This summer I worked for two months as a machine learning intern at FlyRank AI, on a set of search data far too big to fit on my laptop. Somewhere along the way the question I cared about changed from does this work? to can I prove this works?

01 · Curiosity

I take things apart to understand them.

It started with whatever was lying around the house and it never really stopped. The subject changed — it's software now — but the instinct is the same. I can't leave a thing alone until I know why it does what it does.

Half of what I know, I learned by breaking something first.

02 · Honesty

I'd rather be corrected than comfortable.

Being wrong in private costs nothing. Being wrong in public, after everyone has already agreed with me, costs a lot. So I go looking for the hole in my own thinking before anyone else gets the chance to find it.

I'd rather lose the argument than keep the mistake.

03 · Plain words

If I can't say it simply, I don't understand it yet.

Big words are usually somewhere to hide. I'd rather write a sentence that anyone in the room can follow than one that sounds clever and leaves half of them behind. It takes longer to write that way, and it's worth every minute.

Clear beats impressive, every single time.
